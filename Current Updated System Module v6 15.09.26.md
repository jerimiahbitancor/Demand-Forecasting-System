## 2. What the System Does

```
**What ChefDuo Forecast Can Do**
*ChefDuo Forecast combines demand forecasting, product analysis, ingredient demand estimation, and inventory information to provide decision-support insights for daily food service operations.*

**Demand Forecasting**
Analyze historical sales data and generate daily and weekly forecasts for product demand using XGBoost.
**Key information:**
	Historical sales analysis
	Daily forecasts
	Weekly forecasts
	Demand trends
	Forecast accuracy

**Product Performance**
Identify high-, medium-, and low-demand menu items and evaluate product performance against store-level sales patterns.
**Key information:**
	Demand classification
	Best-performing products
	Below-average products
	Product status
	Performance trends
	
**Ingredient Demand**
Estimate future ingredient needs by combining predicted product demand with recipe quantities.
**Key information:**
	Daily ingredient needs
	Weekly ingredient planning
	Recipe-based calculations
	Safety buffer
	Quantity to obtain

**Inventory Management**
Record and monitor ingredient stock information to help compare available inventory with forecasted ingredient needs.
**Key information:**
	Current stock

	Product-ingredient mapping

**Replenishment Support**
Compare forecasted ingredient needs with available stock to identify ingredients that may require replenishment.
**Key information:**
	Critical stock
	Low stock
	Adequate stock
	Excess stock
	Estimated quantity to obtain
	
**Decision Support**
Bring forecasts, product performance, ingredient needs, and inventory information together to support data-driven operational decisions.
**Key information:**
	Forecast insights
	Product trends

	Inventory status
	Replenishment information

## 3. How it Works

```
**How ChefDuo Forecast Works**
ChefDuo Forecast transforms historical sales information into demand forecasts and supply-chain decision-support insights through a series of data-driven steps.

**Step 1 — Upload Sales Data**
	Upload historical sales data exported from your POS system in CSV or XLSX format.
Input: Sales History
↓
**Step 2 — Analyze Demand Patterns**
	The system processes historical sales data and analyzes recurring demand patterns, including time-based sales behavior and product-level trends.
↓
**Step 3 — Generate Demand Forecasts**
	The forecasting model uses processed sales data and relevant forecasting features to generate daily and weekly product demand forecasts.
↓
**Step 4 — Estimate Ingredient Demand**
	Forecasted product quantities are combined with recipe information to estimate the ingredients required for future demand.
↓
**Step 5 — Check Inventory**
	Estimated ingredient needed are compared with recorded inventory quantities to determine the current stock condition.
↓
**Step 6 — Support Replenishment Decisions**
	The system identifies ingredients requiring attention and provides information that helps the business owner determine replenishment needs.
```

## 5. System Scope and Limitation

```
**Built for Decision Support**
ChefDuo Forecast provides data-driven information to support demand planning, inventory monitoring, and replenishment decisions. The system does not automatically purchase ingredients, manage suppliers, or arrange deliveries.

The system supports:
✓ Demand forecasting
✓ Product demand classification
✓ Product performance analysis
✓ Ingredient demand estimation
✓ Inventory monitoring
✓ Replenishment decision support
✓ Data-driven operational insights

The system does not:
✕ Automatically purchase ingredients
✕ Manage suppliers
✕ Process purchase orders
✕ Manage deliveries or logistics
~~✕ Guarantee forecast accuracy~~
✕ Replace business-owner decisions

**Your Data. Your Decisions.**
ChefDuo Forecast uses your provided business data to generate forecasting and decision-support information. Forecast results are estimates and should be interpreted together with actual business conditions and the owner's operational judgment.
```

# Avoid the Strict Upload Rule

Your current rule appears to be:

```
No upload within the day = closed day
```

This should be changed because it may incorrectly classify:

- late uploads,
- failed uploads,
- incomplete POS exports,
- forgotten uploads,
- stores that are still operating,

The revised rule should be:

No upload within the day = upload pending / unconfirmed

Only the following should confirm the date:

# 4. Inventory Management Module

### Inventory Management Functions INCLUDED in This Study

- **Product recipe management** — the owner defines ingredient-to-product mappings (recipes) that specify which ingredients and quantities go into each menu item. These mappings are the foundation for all ingredient demand estimation and COGS computation.
- **Product status auto-computation** — the system automatically derives each product's forecast status from sales history using a 28-day sliding window: Active (sufficient history and recent sales), Inactive — New (insufficient history), or Inactive — Discontinued (no sales in 28 days). Status is recomputed on every data upload and re-evaluated when an archived product is restored.
- **Low Margin detection** — the system identifies products where the profit margin falls below 30% by comparing the selling price against the computed COGS (Note find a studies regarding about the profit margin that falls 30%).These products are flagged with a secondary Low Margin indicator in the product table. Low Margin and Unmapped are mutually exclusive indicators — COGS cannot be computed for unmapped products, so Low Margin cannot be applied to them.
- **Unmapped product and ingredient tracking** — the system tracks products with no ingredient recipe (Unmapped) as a secondary indicator in the Product Management table, and ingredients with no recipe linkage (No Forecast) as a distinct stock status in the Ingredient Management table. Both are surfaced through dedicated preview cards and filter options.
- **Current stock level tracking** — the system maintains a live on-hand quantity per ingredient, updated through sales-triggered automatic deductions and manual restocking entries.
- **Automatic stock deduction** — stock levels decrease automatically when sales data is uploaded, using product-ingredient recipe mappings to calculate the ingredient quantities consumed by the recorded sales.
- **Manual restocking** — the owner can manually record newly received ingredient stock with an optional reason (Purchase, Return, Stock Adjustment, Other) and notes. Every restock entry is recorded in the stock movement history.
- **Forecast-based stock level status** — for mapped ingredients, the system classifies current stock into four tiers by comparing on-hand quantity against tomorrow's forecasted ingredient demand using global percentage thresholds configured in Settings: Critical (stock below 50% of forecasted demand), Low (below 100%), Normal (100–200%), and Excess (above 200%). Ingredients not linked to any product recipe display a No Forecast status since demand cannot be estimated without a recipe.
- **Stock Alerts preview card** — the Ingredient Management preview displays a three-count breakdown of mapped ingredients by stock status (Critical, Low, and remaining), with a direct navigation shortcut to Analytics → Ingredient Demand for the owner to view the full grocery list.
- **COGS computation and display** — ingredient cost per product (COGS) is computed dynamically from the recipe mapping and current unit prices, displayed alongside selling prices in the Product Management table and used to compute margin indicators.
- **Market price reference** — current market reference prices for ingredients are sourced from official Philippine government price monitoring data (Department of Agriculture, Philippine Statistics Authority) and major grocery retailers (SM Supermarket, Puregold, Robinsons Market, etc.) and displayed alongside the owner's recorded unit cost for comparison.
- **Stock movement history** — an auditable log of all stock changes is maintained per ingredient, including automatic sale deductions (Sale — auto), manual restocks (Restock), manual corrections (Adjustment), and recovered stock (Return/Refunded), with timestamp, quantity change, running balance, and source.
- **Archive management** — both products and ingredients can be archived. Products require a reason (Discontinued or Seasonal). The ingredient archive modal displays the affected products linked to the ingredient before confirming, so the owner understands the downstream impact. All archived records retain their full sales and movement history and can be restored at any time. Archived items are hidden from active lists by default and accessible through the Archived filter option.

## Average COGS

Average COGS provides an overall summary of the estimated ingredient costs across the mapped products in the menu.

The system calculates COGS for each product using:

**COGS = Σ (Ingredient Quantity per Serving × Current Ingredient Unit Cost)**

The Average COGS is then calculated using:

**Average COGS = Total COGS of All Eligible Products ÷ Number of Eligible Products**

Only active products with complete ingredient recipes are included in the calculation.

```

## High Food Cost Items

The High Food Cost Items card identifies individual products whose estimated ingredient costs consume a large proportion of their selling price.

The system calculates the Food Cost Percentage using:

**Food Cost Percentage = (COGS ÷ Selling Price) × 100**

A product is flagged as **High Food Cost** when its Food Cost Percentage exceeds the system's configured warning threshold.

### Current Proposed Warning Threshold

**High Food Cost: Food Cost Percentage > 35%**

The threshold functions as a warning indicator to help the owner identify products that may require further review. It does not automatically mean that a product is unprofitable.

For example:

| Product | Selling Price | COGS | Food Cost Percentage | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fried Chicken | ₱100 | ₱25 | 25% | No Warning |
| Pork Chop | ₱100 | ₱35 | 35% | No Warning |
| Adobo | ₱100 | ₱50 | 50% | High Food Cost |
| Special Meal | ₱100 | ₱70 | 70% | High Food Cost |

A product with a high food cost may be reviewed by the owner to determine whether changes to the selling price, recipe, portion size, or ingredient sourcing should be considered.

The High Food Cost indicator does not represent actual net profitability because the system's COGS calculation only includes direct ingredient costs. Other expenses such as labor, rent, utilities, packaging, and other operating costs are not included.

- **ⓘ Information Tooltip** — explains how High Food Cost is calculated.

Product status is **computed automatically** by the system based on sales history. The owner does not set this manually.

   ↓

   ↓

→ Food Cost Percentage cannot be calculated
→ High Food Cost cannot be determined

→ Display HIGH FOOD COST

→ No food cost warning is displayed.

## Cross-Module Notes for Analytics

## Expansion Icon (↗) — Behavior Standard

Every component panel has an ↗ icon in the upper-right corner.

- **Behavior:** Opens a full-screen or large modal showing the complete content of that component — full graph at larger scale, full table (extended pagination 10 rows shows or unpaginated), and export controls.
- **Consistent across:** All panels in Forecasting, Product Performance, and Ingredient Demand.
- **The modal does NOT navigate away** from the current page — it overlays it so the owner can close and return.

## Pagination Standard

- **All tables:** 5 rows visible per page.
- **Controls format:** ← Previous | 1 | 2 | 3 | … | 67 | 68 | Next →
- **Applies to:** Forecast data table, Demand Classification table, Performance Ratio table, all Product Status section tables, Weekly Ingredient heatmap, Daily Ingredient table.
- **Exception:** Grocery List expansion modal — shows all items (no pagination) since it's a print document.

## Market Price Source

- Market prices displayed throughout the system (Ingredient Demand, Grocery List) are sourced from the **Department of Agriculture (DA),  Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)** price, Puregold, Robinsons SuperMarket, etc. but it may vary from major grocery store, monitoring data.
- These are **reference prices**, periodically fetched — not live-streamed.
- This is noted in the Grocery List footer and Daily Ingredient Planner tooltip.
