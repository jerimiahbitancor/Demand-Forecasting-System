6. Ingredient demand, stock status and what to buy

Raw = Σ predicted items sold × recipe amount per item × (1 − modifier share). Safety buffer = Raw × 15%. Forecasted need = Raw + buffer (each rounded to 2 decimals; pieces round up). Predicted Stock Level Status = stock ÷ forecasted need × 100: Critical Shortage below 50%, Insufficient Supply below 100%, Sufficient Supply up to 200%, Above Forecast Limit above 200%. To Buy = forecasted need − stock (only when short). Est. cost = To Buy × unit cost.

Stock, recipes, unit costs and the safety buffer are the values in the system now (when this report ran), exactly like the app's pages. For a past test week, On Stock is today's stock, not the stock on that day.
